Delete your LingoFlow account

LingoFlow: Learn Languages, by Martin Dahl

You can delete your LingoFlow account and its data at any time. The same account is used in the Android app and on the web, so deleting it in one place deletes it everywhere.

In the Android app

  1. Open LingoFlow and go to You, then Settings.
  2. Scroll down to Danger Zone.
  3. Confirm who you are: enter your password, or, if you sign in with Google, type your account email.
  4. Tick I understand this action is permanent and tap Delete Account.

On the web

  1. Sign in at lingoflow.cuplex.se.
  2. Open Setup and scroll down to Danger Zone.
  3. Confirm with your password or your account email, tick the box and select Delete Account.

By email

If you can't sign in, email support@cuplex.se from the address on your account, with the subject “Delete my LingoFlow account”. We'll delete the account within 30 days and reply when it's done. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address first.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes, right away:

  • your account: email address, display name and password hash, or your Google sign-in link;
  • your profile and settings;
  • your progress: lessons, sessions, answers, XP, streaks, achievements, learning path and placement results;
  • your saved words, bookmarks and story history;
  • the exercises you contributed;
  • your subscription status in LingoFlow, and your customer record at RevenueCat, our subscription service.

This can't be undone. If you sign up again later, you start from the beginning.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Database backups can contain your data until they expire, within 30 days.
  • Server logs with your account number are kept for up to 30 days.
  • If you reviewed other people's exercises as a moderator, those exercises stay, without any link to you.
  • Anonymous daily visitor counts aren't linked to your account and stay.

Premium subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Premium subscription bought in Google Play. Cancel it in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, before or after you delete your account. Google keeps its own records of your purchases under the Google Privacy Policy.

Want to delete only some of your data and keep the account? Email support@cuplex.se. See the Privacy Policy for more about the data LingoFlow keeps.
