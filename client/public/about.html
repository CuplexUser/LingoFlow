LingoFlow

Learn a language a few minutes a day

LingoFlow is a language-learning app for the web and Android. It gives you short, focused practice sessions that adapt to what you already know, so you can build vocabulary, grammar and listening skills steadily without planning your own lessons.

What you can learn

Courses in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Russian and Swedish, from complete beginner (CEFR A1) to upper intermediate (B2). Each course covers 15 everyday topics, such as essentials, food and cooking, travel, work, health and conversation.

How it works

  • Daily sessions. Each session mixes multiple-choice questions, sentence building, fill-in-the-blank, flashcards, matching pairs and pronunciation practice.
  • Adaptive review. LingoFlow tracks which words and skills you know and uses spaced repetition to bring back the ones you are about to forget.
  • Stories and exams. Read short stories in the language you are learning, then test yourself with unit exams as you move up through the levels.
  • Progress you can see. XP, streaks, statistics and a knowledge map show what you have learned and what to practice next.

Your account

You need an account so LingoFlow can save your progress and sync it between the web app and the Android app. You can sign up with an email address and password, or with Sign in with Google.

How LingoFlow uses your Google account

When you choose Sign in with Google, LingoFlow asks Google only for your name, your email address and whether that address is verified. We use them for one thing: to create your LingoFlow account and sign you in to it. We don't get access to your Gmail, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account, and we never sell or share this information. See the privacy policy for details, and delete your account at any time.

Price

The web app is free. The Android app is free for beginner (A1) lessons and the daily session, and offers an optional Premium subscription for the higher levels.
