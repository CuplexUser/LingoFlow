Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

This policy explains what data LingoFlow collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the web app at lingoflow.cuplex.se and the LingoFlow app for Android. Both use the same account and the same service.

The short version

  • We collect what LingoFlow needs to work: your account, your settings and your learning progress.
  • There are no ads, no advertising trackers and no analytics SDKs.
  • We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • In the Android app, speech exercises are recognized on your device. We never receive or store recordings of your voice.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the app or on the web. See Delete your account.

Who is responsible

LingoFlow is developed and run by Martin Dahl, Sweden, who is the data controller for the personal data described here. For anything about your data, email support@cuplex.se.

What we collect

Your account

Your email address and display name. If you sign up with a password, we store only a salted hash of it (scrypt), never the password itself. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and name, and confirms that the address is verified; we don't get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account. We also keep short-lived tokens for email verification and password resets.

Your profile and settings

What you enter in Settings: your name, focus area and bio, your native and target languages, your level, and your daily and weekly goals.

Your learning activity

The lessons and practice sessions you start and finish, your answers and which ones were right, your XP, streaks and achievements, your place on the learning path and placement results, the words and exercises you save, and the stories you read. LingoFlow uses this to pick what you practice next and to show your stats.

Exercises you contribute

If you suggest exercises, we store them with your account, along with their review status and any comment from the reviewer. Approved exercises may be shown to other learners, without your name.

Premium subscriptions (Android)

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check them, so RevenueCat and we receive the product, the purchase and expiry dates, whether it renews, and whether there is a billing problem. Payment details stay with Google; we never see your card.

Technical data

Like any web service, our servers see your IP address and basic request details. Our logs record which part of the service was called, the result, how long it took and, when you're signed in, your account number. Sign-in events are logged with a shortened one-way hash of the email address instead of the address itself. To count daily visitors to the sign-in page, we store a salted one-way hash of the IP address, never the address itself.

On your device

The app and the website keep your sign-in token and your preferences (such as theme and voice speed) in your browser's or the app's local storage. LingoFlow uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

Microphone and speech

Speaking exercises use your microphone, only while you're doing one and only after you give permission. You can turn the permission off at any time and skip speaking exercises.

  • Android app: speech is recognized on your device with a built-in speech model. The audio never leaves your device. If the on-device model isn't available, the app can use Android's system speech recognition instead, which is provided by your device's speech service (usually Google) under its own terms.
  • Web app: speech recognition is provided by your browser. Some browsers, such as Chrome, send the audio to their own speech service to recognize it.

In both cases, LingoFlow itself only receives the recognized text, to check your answer.

Spoken audio in lessons is generated from the course text with Google's Gemini speech service and cached. Only course text is sent, never anything about you.

Who processes it for us

We use these service providers to run LingoFlow. They process data only on our behalf and under data processing agreements:

  • Google Cloud (Frankfurt, Germany): runs the LingoFlow API and stores the cached lesson audio.
  • Neon: hosts the database with your account and progress.
  • Vercel: hosts the website.
  • An email delivery provider: sends verification and password reset emails.
  • RevenueCat: checks Google Play subscriptions (Android).

Google is also an independent provider when you choose to sign in with Google or buy a subscription in Google Play; their own privacy policies apply to that.

Some of these providers are based in the United States, so data can be transferred outside the EU and EEA. Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

We may disclose data if the law requires it. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, progress and contributions: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes them from our database right away.
  • Database backups: older copies expire within 30 days.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.
  • Email verification and password reset tokens: until used or expired.
  • Hashed visitor counts: kept as anonymous daily totals.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have incorrect data corrected (most of it you can edit in Settings);
  • have your data deleted, which you can do yourself by deleting your account;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, or have it restricted;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example by turning off the microphone permission.

Email support@cuplex.se from the address on your account and we'll answer within a month. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or the data protection authority where you live.

Security

All traffic between the apps and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, and access to the servers and database is limited to the developer. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data and will tell you without undue delay if a breach puts it at risk.

Children

LingoFlow is meant for people aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
